Apixaban Docking & Sequence Design on NTF2-like Scaffolds

Executive Summary: Apixaban (Factor Xa inhibitor, MW 459.5) was docked into 5 structurally diverse NTF2-like protein scaffolds and 15 pocket-optimized sequences were designed across 3 temperature conditions. The cyanobacterial OCP scaffold (3MG1) showed the strongest predicted binding (Vina score: −8.74 kcal/mol), followed by human G3BP1 NTF2-like domain (4FCJ: −7.24 kcal/mol) and rat NTF2 (1GY6: −6.09 kcal/mol). All designs are available as threaded PDB files and FASTA sequences for downstream experimental validation.

Key Observations

  • 3MG1 (cyanobacterial OCP) has the largest pocket (~15,550 Å³) and the strongest predicted binding (−8.74 kcal/mol). The large pocket may allow more favorable ligand positioning and contact surface.
  • 4FCJ (human G3BP1 NTF2-like) shows good affinity (−7.24 kcal/mol) in a more compact pocket (~3,770 Å³), suggesting a well-complementary binding surface.
  • 2QIY and 2A15 show weaker predicted binding (>−6.0 kcal/mol), with many docked poses clustering far from the best mode, indicating lower pocket complementarity.

Design Temperature Effects

  • T=0.1 (Conservative): Favors residues with the highest complementarity scores. Best for initial experimental screening.
  • T=0.3 (Moderate): Allows more sequence diversity while maintaining most high-scoring substitutions.
  • T=0.5 (Exploratory): Maximum diversity for library-scale screening approaches.
